Privacy Policy for Receipt Vault +

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

Receipt Vault + is designed to help users store and manage receipts on their personal device. Your privacy is important to us.

Overview

Receipt Vault + does not require account creation and does not connect to your bank, credit card, accounting software, advertising networks, or third-party analytics platforms.

Information Stored by the App

The app may store information that you enter or capture, including receipt images, merchant names, receipt amounts, dates, notes, and expense categories.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to help you organize receipts, view summaries, detect potential duplicates, create reports, and export records.

Local Storage and iCloud

Receipt Vault + stores data locally on your device. If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your data may be synced through Apple’s iCloud infrastructure. We do not operate external servers that store or process your receipt data.

Data Sharing

Receipt Vault + does not sell, share, rent, or disclose your receipt information to advertisers, data brokers, financial institutions, or third parties.

Data Deletion

You may delete receipts within the app at any time. If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be removed from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, some data may remain in your iCloud account until removed through Apple’s iCloud settings or from within the app.

Children’s Privacy

Receipt Vault + does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
