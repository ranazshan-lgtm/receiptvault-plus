Questions before you download?

Here are the details users usually want to know before trusting an app with receipt records.

Does Receipt Vault + require an account?

No. Receipt Vault + is designed to work without account creation or sign-in.

Does it connect to my bank or credit card?

No. The app does not connect to banks, credit cards, or accounting platforms.

Where is my data stored?

Your receipt records are stored locally on your device.

Can I export my receipts?

Yes. The app is designed to export clean CSV and PDF records for tax season, reimbursement, bookkeeping, or personal archiving.

Is this a full accounting app?

No. Receipt Vault + is a simple receipt organizer. It is best used alongside your accountant, bookkeeping process, or personal records.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The core receipt archive is designed for local-first use on your device.

How do backups work?

Receipt Vault + allows you to create a backup ZIP containing your receipt data and images. Store the ZIP in Files, iCloud Drive, or another secure location to protect your records.

How do I restore a backup?

Use Restore Backup ZIP from Settings to restore a previously created backup. This can help recover receipts when moving to a new device or after accidental data loss.

What export options are available?

Receipt Vault + supports PDF and CSV exports, allowing you to share records with accountants, bookkeepers, employers, or for your own archiving.

What is included in a CSV export?

CSV exports include receipt details such as merchant, amount, date, category, expense type, and tags. CSV files can be opened in spreadsheet software and imported into many bookkeeping workflows.

Yes. Receipt Vault + is now available on Google Play for Android.

Privacy Policy for Receipt Vault +

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

Overview

The Receipt Vault + app does not require account creation and does not connect to your bank, credit card, accounting software, advertising networks, or third-party analytics platforms.

This website uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics does not access or collect receipt data stored within the app.

Information Stored by the App

The app may store information that you enter or capture, including receipt images, merchant names, receipt amounts, dates, notes, tags, and expense categories.

How Information Is Used

Receipt Vault + currently stores data locally on your device. We do not operate external servers that store or process your receipt data.

Data Deletion

You may delete receipts within the app at any time. If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be removed from your device.
